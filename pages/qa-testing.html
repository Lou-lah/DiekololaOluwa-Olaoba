QUALITY ASSURANCE

QA & Product Testing

I test digital products from both a functional and user perspective, helping identify issues and improve the overall product experience.

MY QA APPROACH

Quality is part of the product, not an afterthought.

My approach to testing is focused on understanding what a product is supposed to do and checking whether it actually delivers that experience.

I look beyond whether a button technically works. I also consider usability, responsiveness, visual consistency and what happens when a user interacts with the product in different ways.

MY WORKFLOW

How I approach a testing task

A structured testing process helps ensure that issues are identified and communicated clearly.

01

Understand Requirements

Understand the product requirements, expected behaviour and user flow.

02

Plan Test Scenarios

Identify important features, user flows and scenarios that need to be tested.

03

Execute Tests

Test the product using expected, unexpected and different user inputs.

04

Record Issues

Document bugs and usability issues with clear reproduction steps and evidence.

05

Retest

Verify fixes after changes have been made and check that existing functionality still works correctly.

BUG REPORTING

What a good bug report should communicate

Bug Title Short and specific description of the issue
Environment Browser, device or platform where the issue occurred
Steps to Reproduce Clear steps another person can follow
Expected Result What should have happened
Actual Result What actually happened
Evidence Screenshot, recording or other supporting information
